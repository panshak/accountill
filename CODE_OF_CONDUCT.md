# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, gender, orientation, or background.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Using sexualized language or imagery
- Personal attacks or trolling
- Public or private harassment

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project team at [ipanshak@gmail.com].
